# Event Manager Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Event Manager is a personal tool used only by its owner. It is not distributed to or used by anyone else.

## What it accesses

Using Google sign-in, it asks for a single permission: to create and manage its own secondary calendar in the owner's Google Calendar account. It cannot see or change any of the owner's other calendars.

## What it does with data

It reads a private list of events stored on the owner's own computer and creates matching events (titles, dates, times, notes and reminders) in that secondary calendar. Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.

## Sharing and retention

No data is sent to any server operated by the developer or to any third party, other than to Google Calendar itself. Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising. The owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
